The Internet connects people and devices worldwide. It is a place to find information and communicate with people anywhere. This module covers the types of networks, how data is represented and sent, and how transfer speed is measured.

* * *

## 1. Network Types

Networks come in all sizes, from two devices to millions. Communicating over a network is usually **more efficient and less expensive** than traditional forms of communication.

### Local Networks

- Small home networks
- SOHO (Small Office / Home Office)
- Medium to large networks
- Worldwide network (the **Internet**)

### Mobile Devices

- Smartphones
- Tablets
- Smartwatches
- Smart glasses

### Connected Home Devices

- Security systems
- Appliances
- Smart TVs
- Gaming consoles

### Other Connected Devices

- Smart cars
- RFID tags
- Sensors and actuators
- Medical devices

* * *

## 2. Data Transmission

### Types of Personal Data

| Type | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Volunteered data** | Data I offer myself (e.g. posting on social media) |
| **Observed data** | Captured by recording an individual's activity (e.g. location data) |
| **Inferred data** | Data worked out by analysing volunteered or observed data (e.g. a credit score) |

### The Bit

- Computers store and send data as **binary digits (bits)**: `0` and `1`
- These represent **off** and **on**
- Characters are converted to bits using codes such as **ASCII**

* * *

## 3. Common Methods of Data Transmission

| Method | How data is sent |
|--------|------------------|
| **Electrical signals** | Electrical pulses on copper wire |
| **Optical signals** | Electrical signals converted into light pulses |
| **Wireless signals** | Infrared, microwave, or radio waves through the air |

* * *

## 4. Bandwidth and Throughput

Data can be transferred at different speeds. This rate is described using **bandwidth** and **throughput**.

### Bandwidth

- The **capacity** of a medium to carry data in a given amount of time
- Measured in: **kbps, Mbps, Gbps, Tbps**

### Throughput

- The **actual amount of data** sent and received over a connection
- Includes any delays that occur, so it is usually lower than bandwidth

* * *

## Key Takeaways

- Networks range from small home setups to the global Internet.
- Many devices are now connected: phones, home devices, cars, sensors, and medical devices.
- Personal data can be volunteered, observed, or inferred.
- All data is stored and sent as bits (0 and 1).
- Data travels as electrical, optical, or wireless signals.
- Bandwidth is the capacity of a connection; throughput is the actual data transferred.
