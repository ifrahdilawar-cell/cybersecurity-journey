## Overview
This module covered legal and ethical issues in cybersecurity, along with education paths and career opportunities in the field. It highlights how cybersecurity professionals must operate within laws while developing technical and analytical skills.

---

## ⚖️ 5.1 Legal and Ethical Issues in Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity activities must follow strict legal and ethical guidelines. Any unauthorized activity can lead to legal consequences.

---

### 👤 Personal Legal Issues
- Unauthorized system access  
- Identity theft  
- Cyber harassment  
- Unauthorized use of hacking tools  

---

### 🏢 Corporate Legal Issues
- Data breaches  
- Poor data protection practices  
- Non-compliance with regulations  
- Weak access control policies  

---

### 🌍 Government / International Legal Issues
- Cross-border cybercrime  
- Jurisdiction conflicts  
- International cyber law enforcement  
- Cyber warfare regulations  

---

## 🎓 5.2 Education and Careers in Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity offers multiple learning paths and career opportunities across offensive and defensive security domains.

---

### 🛡️ CEH (Certified Ethical Hacker)
Focuses on ethical hacking techniques, penetration testing concepts, and understanding attacker methodologies to secure systems.

---

### 🔐 CISSP (Certified Information Systems Security Professional)
Advanced certification covering security management, risk analysis, and enterprise-level cybersecurity governance.

---

### 🧠 CompTIA Security+
Entry-level certification covering basic cybersecurity principles, threats, vulnerabilities, and foundational security practices.

---

### ☁️ CCNA Cyber Ops / Cisco Certifications
Focuses on networking fundamentals, security operations, and monitoring techniques used in SOC environments.

---

### 🔎 OSCP (Offensive Security Certified Professional)
Hands-on penetration testing certification focused on real-world exploitation techniques and advanced offensive security skills.

---

📌 Cybersecurity is a fast-growing field with strong global demand.

---

## 🧠 Key Takeaways
- Cybersecurity work must follow strict legal and ethical rules
- Unauthorized access is illegal regardless of intent
- Companies are responsible for protecting sensitive data
- Cybercrime is global but governed by local laws
- Multiple career paths exist in cybersecurity (Red & Blue teams)
