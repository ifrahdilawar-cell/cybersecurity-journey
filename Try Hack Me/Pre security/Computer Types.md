## Status

Completed ✅

## What I Learned

* Computers come in different forms depending on their purpose.
* Each type is designed with specific trade-offs.
* There is no single computer that is best for every task.

---

## Key Concepts

### Laptop

* Portable computer.
* Includes a screen and keyboard.
* Designed for everyday computing.

---

### Desktop

* Fixed-location computer.
* Includes a screen and keyboard.
* Provides sustained performance.

---

### Workstation

* High-performance computer.
* Designed for professional workloads.
* Focuses on reliability and precision.

---

### Server

* Provides services to multiple users.
* Usually does not have a screen or keyboard.
* Designed for reliability and availability.

---

### Smartphone

* Pocket-sized computer.
* Optimized for battery life and connectivity.

Examples:

* iPhone
* Android phones

---

### Tablet

* Touch-based computer.
* Larger screen than a smartphone.

Examples:

* iPad
* Drawing tablets

---

### IoT Devices

* Network-connected devices with a specific purpose.
* Can send and receive data.

Examples:

* Smart thermostat
* Smart doorbell
* Fitness tracker

---

### Embedded Computers

* Built into another device.
* Performs a specific task.
* May not have network connectivity.

Examples:

* Coffee maker controller
* Automatic door sensor
* Lamp dimmer chip

---

### IoT vs Embedded

**IoT Devices**

* Connected to a network.
* Can report data and receive commands.

**Embedded Computers**

* May not connect to a network.
* Operate inside another device.

---

### Design Trade-Offs

Different computers are built for different needs.

* Mobility reduces performance.
* Reliability increases cost.
* Purpose determines design.

Examples:

* Phones prioritize portability.
* Servers prioritize reliability.
* IoT devices work quietly in the background.

## Takeaway

There is no "best" computer. Every computer is designed for a specific purpose, and choosing the right tool depends on the job.
